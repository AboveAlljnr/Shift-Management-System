# Product Requirements

See the approved V1 Product Requirements Specification for the complete product definition.

## Core capabilities

- Multi-tenant company management
- Branches, departments, teams, and employees
- Role-based and scoped permissions
- Shift templates and scheduling
- Staffing requirements, skills, and certifications
- Availability and leave
- Attendance, breaks, GPS/geofencing
- Activities and training
- AI-assisted scheduling
- Documents
- Notifications and announcements
- Reports and analytics
- Subscriptions and billing
- Super Admin
- Security, privacy, auditing, backups, and disaster recovery
- Responsive web/PWA with offline attendance

## Product principle

AI assists managers; it does not publish schedules autonomously.
